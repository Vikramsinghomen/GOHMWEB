About GreatOhm

GreatOhm was born from a simple vision — to bring every occult science together in one sacred, beautifully crafted space. We believe that ancient wisdom should be accessible to everyone, in a modern and intuitive way.

Our Mission

Our mission is to unify astrology, numerology, tarot, Vastu, palmistry and more into a single seamless experience, empowering users with insight, guidance and clarity without toggling multiple apps.

What Makes GreatOhm Different

  • A: Every major occult science in one app — the true "Omni" experience.
  • B: Personalised, data-driven insights tailored to your unique birth chart.
  • C: A serene, intuitive interface designed for daily use.
  • D: Privacy-first approach to your personal and birth data.

Our Values

  • A: Authenticity — honouring the traditions and sciences we present.
  • B: Accessibility — making wisdom easy for everyone.
  • C: Integrity — honest guidance with clear disclaimers.
  • D: Innovation — continuously evolving with new tools and sciences.
