About GreatOhm

GreatOhm was born from a simple observation — that the tools people turn to for spiritual guidance and self-discovery were scattered across many separate apps. One app for astrology, another for numerology, third for tarot, fourth for Vaastu. Each required the same birth details to be entered again and again, held different policies, and rarely spoke to one another.

GreatOhm began with a single vision: to bring every occult science together in one sacred, beautifully crafted space. We believe ancient wisdom should be accessible to everyone in a modern and intuitive way — not locked inside jargon, paywalls or a dozen disconnected downloads.

What GreatOhm Is

GreatOhm is a Vedic-life-guidance app that unifies Vedic astrology, numerology, Vaastu Shastra, palmistry, tarot, Vedic calendar information, Nakshatra analysis and related tools under one account. You provide your profile once — date, time and place of birth with timezone handling, your name and location — and the app reuses those details to generate reports, charts and predictions across every service. Where relevant, outputs can be exported as beautiful PDF reports.

Our Mission

Our mission is to unify astrology, numerology, tarot, Vaastu, palmistry and more into a single seamless experience, empowering users with insight, guidance and clarity without toggling multiple apps. We want each reading to feel personal, respectful and honest — not generic.

What Makes GreatOhm Different

Several things set GreatOhm apart from a typical astrology or numerology app:

  • The true "Omni" experience — every major occult science in one app, from daily horoscopes and kundli matching to AI palm reading and Vaastu floor-plan tools.
  • The 360-degree Ohm Score — a daily 0–100 composite reflecting your activity across services, with a per-area breakdown and trends that feed personalised remedy recommendations.
  • Personalised, data-driven insights tailored to your unique birth chart, your numbers and your hand — not one-size-fits-all content.
  • Live consultations with verified experts — Pandit ji, numerologists, Vaastu experts, palmists and tarot readers over masked calls.
  • A serene, intuitive interface designed for daily use.
  • A privacy-first approach to your personal and birth data.

Our Values

  • Authenticity — honouring the traditions and sciences we present.
  • Accessibility — making wisdom easy for everyone, in 26 languages.
  • Integrity — honest guidance with clear disclaimers.
  • Innovation — continuously evolving with new tools, sciences and AI capabilities.
