The 50 Tarot Questions Indians Ask Most — Answered

Tarot By GreatOhm · Updated 2026-10-10

Tarot has become hugely popular in India, both through online readers and the cards people buy to try at home. But it comes with real worries: Is tarot accurate? Can I read it for myself? Which deck should I buy? Is tarot connected to black magic or bad luck? Drawing on the questions Indian seekers ask most (from the popular tarot FAQs to the everyday concerns about love, career and safety), here are the 50 that come up again and again — answered calmly. Remember: tarot is a reflective tool, not a proven predictor, and nothing here replaces professional medical, financial or legal advice.

Tarot Basics & Common Worries

What tarot is, how it works, and the fears that come up first.

What is tarot card reading?

Tarot is a divination practice that uses a deck of symbolic cards to offer insight and prompt reflection. You focus on a question, draw cards, and interpret their imagery and meaning to explore a situation.

How accurate is tarot?

Tarot is widely regarded as a pseudoscience — its predictions are not supported by evidence. Many people still find readings accurate-feeling because the cards prompt honest self-reflection. Treat it as guidance, not fact.

Can tarot really predict my future?

No. Tarot does not give exact predictions. It reflects your current situation, patterns and possible directions, helping you think more clearly — your choices shape what actually happens.

Is tarot connected to black magic or evil?

No. Tarot is a tool for reflection used by people of many faiths and none. It has no inherent link to black magic, and the "evil" idea is a cultural myth, not part of the practice.

Is tarot against my religion?

Tarot itself is secular, though some religious traditions discourage divination. This is a personal choice — many people use tarot purely as a journaling and self-reflection tool.

Can tarot be dangerous or negative?

The cards are not dangerous. The only risk is becoming anxious or dependent on readings — so use them for reflection, and avoid readers who use fear to sell expensive remedies.

Does tarot work only for believers?

You do not need to "believe" for a reading to be a useful prompt. Whether or not you see it as mystical, the reflection it encourages can still be helpful.

Is tarot the same as astrology?

They are different tools. Astrology maps the planets at your birth; tarot draws cards for a present question. Both aim at insight, and some readers combine them.

How many cards are in a tarot deck?

A standard deck has 78 cards: 22 Major Arcana (big life themes) and 56 Minor Arcana (everyday situations, in four suits).

Is online tarot as good as in-person?

A thoughtful reading depends on the interpretation, not the format. Online and app-based readings can be just as reflective, and are often more private and convenient.

The Deck: Arcana, Suits & Reversed Cards

Understanding the 78 cards before you read them.

What is the difference between Major and Minor Arcana?

The 22 Major Arcana cards mark big life themes and turning points (like The Fool, The Lovers, Death). The 56 Minor Arcana cover everyday situations across four suits.

What do the four tarot suits mean?

Cups relate to emotions and relationships; Pentacles to money, work and material matters; Swords to thoughts, communication and conflict; Wands to passion, creativity and action.

Which element does each suit represent?

Cups = water, Pentacles = earth, Swords = air, Wands = fire. Many readers use these elements to understand how a suit's energy behaves.

What are court cards?

The 16 court cards (Page, Knight, Queen and King in each suit) represent personalities, roles or stages of maturity — sometimes an actual person, sometimes an aspect of you.

What does a reversed tarot card mean?

A reversal is not bad luck. It usually means the card's energy is blocked, delayed or turned inward — a nuance to explore, not a curse.

Is a reversed card always negative?

No. Reversals often show inner processing, hesitation or a lesson still unfolding. They add depth rather than predicting misfortune.

Do I have to use reversals?

It is optional. Many beginners start with upright-only readings and add reversals once comfortable. Both approaches are valid.

Which tarot deck is best for beginners in India?

The Rider-Waite-Smith deck is the most widely recommended for beginners because its imagery matches most guidebooks, making it easy to learn.

Are there Indian-themed tarot decks?

Yes — many artists create decks with Indian deities, mythology or festivals. Choose imagery that resonates with you, as connection makes reading more intuitive.

What is the most famous tarot card?

Cards like Death, The Tower and The Lovers are the best known. Death rarely means literal death — it usually signals transformation and an ending that makes way for something new.

How to Read, Daily Pulls & Yes/No Tarot

The practical "how do I actually do this?" questions.

Can I read tarot for myself?

Yes — many people read for themselves, especially for reflection. It can be harder to stay objective about your own situation, so keep questions focused and honest.

How do I ask a good tarot question?

Ask open, empowering questions like "What can I do to improve this situation?" rather than "Will X happen?" Open questions give the cards room to offer guidance instead of a yes/no.

What is a one-card daily pull?

Drawing a single card each morning as a theme or focus for the day. It is the simplest, most popular practice and a gentle way to learn the cards.

How often should I pull a tarot card?

A daily one-card pull is fine, but for bigger questions avoid repeating the same question obsessively. Give situations time to unfold between readings.

What is a three-card spread?

A simple spread often read as past-present-future or situation-action-outcome. It is a great step up from a one-card pull and very beginner-friendly.

What is the Celtic Cross spread?

A classic ten-card spread that explores a situation in depth — the core issue, influences, hopes, fears and likely outcome. It is best once you know the cards well.

How does a yes/no tarot reading work?

A focused pull to clarify a decision. Rather than a rigid answer, it examines the energy around your situation so you can make your own informed choice.

How do I shuffle and draw cards properly?

There is no single right way. Shuffle while focusing on your question, then cut and draw the number of cards your spread needs — what matters most is your intention.

Do I need to cleanse my tarot deck?

Many readers like to cleanse a new or heavily-used deck to "reset" it — with smoke, moonlight, crystals or simply shuffling. It is a personal ritual, not a requirement.

Where should I keep my tarot cards?

Store them somewhere clean and special — a box or cloth. Many readers believe handling them with respect keeps the practice meaningful.

Love, Career, Money Tarot & Choosing a Reader

How Indians apply tarot to real questions — and how to choose wisely.

Can tarot help with my love life?

Tarot is often used to reflect on relationships. Cards like The Lovers, Two of Cups or Ace of Cups highlight connection and new romance — but the reading is a prompt for reflection, not a guarantee.

Which tarot card is lucky for love?

The Lovers, Two of Cups and Ace of Cups are traditionally seen as positive love cards, symbolising attraction, harmony and new beginnings.

Can tarot guide my career choice?

Yes — many use tarot to explore career options and mindset. The Pentacles suit especially relates to work and money. Use it to think clearly, alongside practical planning.

Can tarot tell me about money and wealth?

Tarot can prompt reflection on your money habits and options, but it cannot predict wealth. Never rely on a reading for financial or investment decisions.

Will tarot tell me when I will get married?

It will not give a date. Tarot can help you reflect on what you want and what is blocking you, but marriage timing is shaped by real-life choices, not cards.

Can tarot tell me about my health?

No — tarot cannot diagnose or predict health outcomes. For any medical concern, always consult a qualified doctor. Tarot may help you reflect on stress or self-care.

Should I consult a professional tarot reader?

It can be helpful for a fresh perspective. Choose someone ethical who empowers you rather than frightening you or pushing costly remedies.

How do I know if a tarot reader is genuine?

A genuine reader is compassionate, does not guarantee exact outcomes, avoids fear tactics, and encourages your own decision-making rather than dependence.

Can I learn tarot on my own?

Absolutely. Start with a beginner-friendly deck and its guidebook, learn one card a day, and keep a tarot journal. Many self-taught readers become very skilled.

How is tarot different from numerology or astrology?

All are reflective tools, but tarot draws cards for a present question, numerology uses your birth date and name, and astrology maps the planets at your birth. Tarot needs no birth details at all.

Card Meanings, Spreads & Everyday Doubts

Popular cards, common spreads, and the questions that come up as you practise.

What does The Fool tarot card mean?

The Fool symbolises new beginnings, spontaneity and a leap of faith. It often appears when you are starting something new and trusting the journey.

What does The Tower card mean?

The Tower represents sudden change or a revelation that shakes things up. It is uncomfortable but often clears the way for a more honest foundation.

What does The Star card mean?

The Star is a hopeful card of healing, renewal and guidance after difficulty — a sign that calm and inspiration are returning.

What does The Lovers card represent?

Beyond romance, The Lovers is about meaningful choices, alignment and union — a significant relationship or an important decision of the heart.

Are there oracle cards as well as tarot?

Yes. Oracle decks have their own themes and varying card counts, often simpler than tarot. Many people use both — tarot for structure, oracle for inspiration.

What are runes and I-Ching in tarot apps?

They are other divination tools sometimes bundled with tarot: runes are ancient symbols cast for guidance, and the I-Ching uses coins or yarrow sticks for wisdom on change.

Can two people get a couples tarot reading?

Yes — many readers do relationship spreads to explore how two people relate. It is a conversation starter for reflection, not a verdict on the relationship.

Does the time of day matter for tarot?

It is a personal preference. Some readers feel calmer at night; others read in the morning. Choose a time when you can focus without distraction.

Why do I keep drawing the same card?

Repeated cards often highlight a lesson or theme you have not yet addressed. Readers see it as the deck emphasising something important in your life right now.

Can I mix tarot with my other spiritual practices?

Yes — many people combine tarot with meditation, journaling, astrology or prayer. It works well as a reflective companion to almost any practice.
