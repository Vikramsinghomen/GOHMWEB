Sade Sati Check — Know Your Saturn Phase

Sade Sati is one of the most discussed periods in Vedic astrology. GreatOhm tells you instantly whether you are in Sade Sati, which of the three phases you are passing through, and what remedies suit your chart.

What is Sade Sati?

Sade Sati, literally "seven and a half", is the roughly 7.5-year period when Saturn (Shani) transits the twelfth, first and second houses from the natal Moon sign. Because the Moon governs the mind, Saturn's movement over and around it is considered a significant period of karma, discipline and transformation.

The Three Phases of Sade Sati

Sade Sati is traditionally divided into three distinct phases, each lasting about 2.5 years:

  • Rising phase (Shani in the 12th house): Saturn approaches the Moon. Often brings expenses, travel, and a turning away from past habits.
  • Peak phase (Shani in the 1st house): Saturn sits over the Moon sign. This is the most intense phase — health, confidence and responsibilities are tested, and results of past actions surface.
  • Setting phase (Shani in the 2nd house): Saturn moves away. Family, speech and finances come into focus, and the pressure gradually releases.

Is Sade Sati Always Difficult?

Not at all. Saturn is the great teacher. Many individuals report significant career growth, spiritual maturity and life clarity during Sade Sati. Its effect depends heavily on where Saturn sits in the birth chart, its dignity, and how you respond to its lessons. Knowing your phase helps you navigate it with awareness instead of fear.
