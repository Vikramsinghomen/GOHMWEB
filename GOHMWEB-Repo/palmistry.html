What is Palmistry?

Palmistry, also called chiromancy, is the ancient practice of reading the hands. The lines, mounts, shapes and markings of your palm are believed to reflect your personality, talents and the path your life may take. It is one of the oldest and most intuitive of the occult sciences.

The Major Palm Lines

Every palm tells a unique story through its lines. GreatOhm guides you through the most significant ones:

  • Heart line — emotions, love and relationships
  • Head line — intellect, mindset and decision-making
  • Life line — vitality, energy and major life changes
  • Fate line — career, purpose and life direction

Mounts & Shapes

Beyond the lines, the mounts of your palm — the fleshy areas beneath each finger — and the overall shape of your hand add depth to a reading. GreatOhm explains these elements in clear, beginner-friendly language so you can begin reading palms with confidence.

A Guide for Beginners & Beyond

Whether you are just learning to identify the major lines or exploring deeper combinations and markings, GreatOhm's palmistry guides grow with you. Simple, visual and beautifully designed, they make this ancient science accessible to everyone.
