What is Numerology?

Numerology is the ancient study of the hidden meaning behind numbers. Every number carries a vibration, and by understanding the numbers connected to your birth date and name, you can gain insight into your personality, strengths, challenges and life purpose.

Life Path Number

Your life path number is the most important number in numerology. Calculated from your full date of birth, it reveals the direction your life is likely to take and the lessons you are here to learn. GreatOhm calculates it instantly and explains each number's meaning in depth.

Destiny & Name Numerology

Your destiny (or expression) number is derived from the letters of your full birth name. It reflects the talents, abilities and inner goals you carry into the world. GreatOhm also offers name numerology so you can explore the vibration of your current name or a potential new one.

Mobile Number Numerology

In modern numerology practice, even your mobile number carries a subtle influence. GreatOhm provides detailed interpretations for mobile number numerology, helping you understand the energy attached to the digits you use every day.

More Than Just Numbers

Numerology is most powerful when combined with other occult sciences. In GreatOhm, your numerology readings work alongside your Vedic birth chart, tarot pulls and Vastu guidance for a complete picture of your life's path.
