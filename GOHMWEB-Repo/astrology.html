What Makes a Great Astrology App?

A truly useful astrology app goes beyond a generic daily horoscope. It understands your exact birth time, birthplace and birth date, and interprets the planetary positions that shape your personality, relationships, career and life path. GreatOhm was built with this depth in mind — combining authentic Vedic tradition with a modern, effortless experience.

Vedic Astrology at the Core

GreatOhm is rooted in Vedic (Jyotish) astrology, one of the world's oldest and most precise astrological systems. Instead of brief, surface-level readings, you get:

  • Complete Vedic birth chart (Kundli) generation
  • Planetary dashas and yogas explained clearly
  • Daily, weekly and monthly horoscope readings
  • Real planetary positions and Hindu panchang data
  • Kundli matching for marriage and compatibility

Personalised Predictions & Remedies

Because your chart is unique, your guidance should be too. GreatOhm uses your birth details to deliver personalised predictions and gentle remedies — mantras, rituals and gemstone recommendations — tailored to your planetary profile. Your private birth data stays encrypted, protected and yours alone.
