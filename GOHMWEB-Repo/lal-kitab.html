Lal Kitab Remedies — Simple, Practical Solutions

Lal Kitab is famous for remedies that are simple, inexpensive and remarkably practical. GreatOhm brings Lal Kitab-based astrological solutions into the app, with self-serve reading and structured remedy lists.

Understanding Lal Kitab

Lal Kitab is a distinctive school of Vedic astrology composed in the early 20th century. What makes it unique is its system — the same planet or house can behave differently depending on the chart, and remedies are prescribed to balance those relationships. It emphasises everyday actions over expensive rituals.

How Remedies Are Determined

In GreatOhm, your birth chart is interpreted in the Lal Kitab style — the twelve houses are examined together with their planetary occupants and signs. Remedies are then matched to the weak points, following classical Lal Kitab principles such as:

  • Feeding grains or water to birds and animals on specific days
  • Donating jaggery, wheat, or other items to those in need
  • Lighting a mustard-oil lamp at specific times
  • Wearing or placing simple items associated with a planet

Problems Commonly Addressed

Lal Kitab remedies are frequently applied to marriage delays, financial strain, health concerns, property and career matters, as well as planetary conditions such as Mangal Dosha and Kaal Sarp Dosha. Each remedy is tied to a specific planetary or house weakness in the chart.
