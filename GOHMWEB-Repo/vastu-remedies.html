Vastu Remedies — Balancing Your Space

Vastu is the ancient Indian science of balancing the five elements and directions in a living space. When a home has a dosha — an imbalance — simple remedies can often restore harmony without demolition or renovation.

What Is a Vastu Dosha?

A vastu dosha occurs when the elements, directions and functions of a space are out of balance. Common examples include a blocked northeast (the zone of prosperity), a kitchen in the northeast, heavy beams over beds, or mirrors facing the main door. Doshas are read against the compass orientation of the site.

Remedies That Need No Building Work

The good news is that most doshas can be softened with non-structural remedies:

  • Keeping the northeast quadrant the lightest and most open part of the home
  • Placing copper vessels, pyramids, or element-specific items in affected zones
  • Correcting mirror placements so they do not reflect bedrooms or the entrance
  • Using direction-appropriate colours — cool tones in the northeast, warm tones in the south and southwest
  • Relocating water features, plants and heavy furniture along correct walls

Room-by-Room Guidance

Bedrooms, kitchens, pooja rooms and study spaces each follow their own vastu logic. The kitchen favours the southeast; the master bedroom the southwest; the pooja room the northeast; and the study ideally the west or northeast. GreatOhm gives room-specific guidance instead of one-size-fits-all rules.
