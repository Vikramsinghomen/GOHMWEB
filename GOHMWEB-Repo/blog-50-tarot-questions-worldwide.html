The 50 Tarot Questions People Search For Worldwide — Answered

Tarot By GreatOhm · Updated 2026-10-10

Tarot is one of the world's most-searched spiritual topics, with roots going back to 15th-century Italy. Most searches are refreshingly simple: What is tarot? What do the cards mean? How do I read them? Can I read my own? Is it real? Drawing on the most-read beginner guides, here are the 50 questions people look up most — answered clearly, with the honest reminder that tarot is a symbolic, reflective practice rather than proven prediction.

Tarot Basics & the Big Questions

What tarot is, how the deck is structured, and the honest "is it real?" question.

What is tarot?

Tarot is a deck of 78 illustrated cards used for reflection and insight. You focus on a question, draw cards, and interpret their symbols and meanings to explore a situation.

What is the difference between tarot and a normal card deck?

A tarot deck has 78 cards including 22 extra "trump" cards (the Major Arcana) with rich symbolism, unlike the 52-card deck used for games.

What are the Major Arcana?

The 22 Major Arcana cards (The Fool through The World) represent big life themes, lessons and turning points — the "heart" of the tarot deck.

What are the Minor Arcana?

The 56 Minor Arcana cards deal with everyday matters. They are divided into four suits — Wands, Cups, Swords and Pentacles — much like a regular playing deck.

What do the four suits represent?

Wands = passion and action (fire); Cups = emotions and relationships (water); Swords = thoughts and communication (air); Pentacles = money and material life (earth).

What are court cards?

The 16 court cards — Page, Knight, Queen and King in each suit — represent people, personalities or stages of maturity within a reading.

Is tarot real?

Tarot is widely viewed as a pseudoscience, since its predictions lack evidence. Many people still find it valuable as a tool for reflection, creativity and self-understanding.

Can tarot predict the future?

It does not give exact predictions. Tarot reflects your present situation and possible directions, helping you think more clearly — the future is shaped by your choices.

Is tarot connected to religion or the occult?

Tarot has been used in various spiritual and esoteric traditions, but it is not tied to any one religion. Many people use it purely as a reflective, creative tool.

Is tarot evil or dangerous?

No. The cards are simply printed images. Tarot is not inherently dangerous — the only risk is anxiety or over-dependence, which comes from how you use it, not the cards themselves.

Where did tarot come from?

Tarot decks first appeared in 15th-century Italy as playing cards, and were later adopted for divination in France from the 18th century onward.

What is cartomancy?

Cartomancy is fortune-telling with cards in general. Tarot reading is one popular form of it, but cartomancy can also use ordinary playing cards.

Do different tarot decks have different meanings?

The core structure and traditional meanings are shared, but each deck's artwork adds its own flavour. Beginners do best sticking to one deck's guidebook while learning.

What is a "significator" in tarot?

A significator is a card chosen to represent you (or the person asking) in a reading, often selected based on age and personality.

Reversed Cards & Popular Card Meanings

The two most-searched details: what a reversed card means, and what the famous cards say.

What does a reversed tarot card mean?

A reversal usually means the card's energy is blocked, delayed or turned inward. It is a nuance to explore, not a sign of bad luck.

Are reversed cards bad?

No. Reversals often show inner processing or a lesson still developing. Many readers find them deeper and more insightful than upright cards.

Do I have to read reversals?

It is optional. Beginners often start with upright-only and add reversals later once they know the cards well.

What does The Fool mean?

The Fool symbolises new beginnings, spontaneity and taking a leap of faith — the start of a journey with unknown but exciting possibilities.

What does The Magician represent?

The Magician is about manifestation, focus and using your skills to turn ideas into reality — a card of empowered action.

What does The Lovers card mean?

Beyond romance, The Lovers is about meaningful choices, harmony and alignment — a significant relationship or an important decision of the heart.

What does Death mean in tarot?

Death almost never means literal death. It signals transformation, endings and the close of one chapter so a new one can begin.

What does The Tower card mean?

The Tower represents sudden upheaval or a revelation that shakes your foundations — uncomfortable, but often clearing the way for something more honest.

What does The Star card mean?

The Star is a hopeful card of healing, renewal and quiet inspiration after a difficult time — calm and guidance are returning.

What does The World card represent?

The World signals completion, achievement and wholeness — the successful end of a cycle and readiness for the next.

What does the Ace of Cups mean?

The Ace of Cups symbolises new love, emotional beginnings and an overflowing of feeling — a fresh start in the heart.

What does the Three of Swords mean?

The Three of Swords often points to heartbreak, painful truth or difficult news — but it also marks the honesty needed to heal and move on.

What does the Ten of Pentacles represent?

The Ten of Pentacles is about lasting abundance, family legacy and long-term security — wealth that is shared and endures.

What does the Eight of Wands mean?

The Eight of Wands signals swift movement, messages and things finally falling into place quickly after a period of waiting.

How to Read, Spreads & Daily Pulls

The practical "how do I actually do this?" questions.

Can you read tarot for yourself?

Yes — self-readings are common and great for reflection. It can be harder to stay objective, so keep your questions focused and honest.

How do I do a tarot reading?

Calm your mind, hold a question, shuffle, then draw cards into a spread and interpret them together. Intention matters more than a rigid procedure.

What is a tarot spread?

A spread is a set layout of positions, each with a meaning. Common ones include the one-card pull, three-card spread and the ten-card Celtic Cross.

What is a three-card tarot spread?

A simple three-card layout often read as past-present-future or situation-action-outcome — a great beginner spread.

What is the Celtic Cross spread?

A classic ten-card spread that explores a situation in depth — the core issue, influences, hopes, fears and likely outcome.

What is a daily one-card pull?

Drawing a single card each day as a theme or focus. It is the simplest practice and a gentle way to learn the deck.

How often should I read tarot?

A daily one-card pull is fine, but avoid repeating the same big question obsessively. Give things time to unfold between deeper readings.

How do I ask a good tarot question?

Ask open, empowering questions — "What can I do to improve this?" rather than "Will X happen?" Open questions give richer, more useful guidance.

Do I need to cleanse my tarot deck?

Many readers like to reset a new or heavily-used deck using smoke, moonlight, crystals or shuffling. It is a personal ritual, not a requirement.

How should I store my tarot cards?

Keep them somewhere clean and special, like a box or cloth. Treating them with care keeps the practice meaningful to you.

Choosing a Deck, Getting Started & Doubts

Picking your first deck, learning fast, and the honest limits.

Which tarot deck should a beginner choose?

The Rider-Waite-Smith deck is the most recommended for beginners — its imagery matches most guidebooks, making it easy to learn.

Are there other beginner-friendly decks?

Yes — many modern decks are designed for beginners with plain-language guidebooks. Choose art that resonates with you, since connection makes reading more intuitive.

What is the difference between tarot and oracle cards?

Tarot follows a set 78-card structure; oracle decks are more freeform with their own themes and varying counts. Many people use both.

How long does it take to learn tarot?

You can start reading simple spreads within days by learning one card at a time. Fluency grows over months of regular practice.

Do I need psychic abilities to read tarot?

No. Tarot relies on learning the card meanings and using intuition and reflection — skills anyone can develop with practice.

Can tarot help with anxiety or decisions?

It can be a reflective tool to organise your thoughts, but it is not therapy. For persistent anxiety, seek support from a qualified mental-health professional.

Is it bad to pull a "scary" card like Death or The Tower?

No. These cards signal change and transformation, not disaster. Readers see them as challenging but ultimately clearing the way for growth.

Can I get a tarot reading online or from an app?

Yes — online and app readings can be just as reflective as in-person ones, and are often more private, instant and convenient.

Should I pay for a professional tarot reading?

It can offer a fresh perspective. Choose an ethical reader who empowers you rather than frightening you or pushing costly extras.

How can I start learning tarot today?

Get a beginner deck, learn one card a day, and keep a tarot journal. Apps like GreatOhm also let you practise daily pulls and spreads digitally while you learn.

Can tarot and astrology be used together?

Yes — many readers pair a tarot pull with a birth chart, using astrology for timing and tarot for a focused question. They complement each other nicely.

What is a "pull" versus a full "spread"?

A pull is one or a few cards for a quick check-in; a spread is a structured layout for a deeper question. Beginners can get a lot from simple pulls.
