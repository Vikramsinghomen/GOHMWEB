The 50 Vaastu & Feng Shui Questions People Search For Worldwide — Answered

Vastu By GreatOhm · Updated 2026-10-10

Around the world, people search for the same thing Indian families ask a Vaastu consultant: how do I make my home feel calm, balanced and lucky? In the West this often arrives as "feng shui," while in India it is "Vaastu Shastra." Drawing on the most-searched beginner questions (what is chi, what is the bagua map, where should my bed and front door be) and the widely-read comparisons between the two traditions, here are the 50 questions people look up most — answered plainly, with the reminder that good design, light and safety always come first.

Vaastu & Feng Shui: The Basics

The two great traditions of arranging space, and what they actually are.

What is Vaastu Shastra?

Vaastu is the ancient Indian science of architecture and spatial harmony, over 5,000 years old and rooted in the Vedas. It guides how buildings are designed and rooms placed to align with nature, direction and the five elements.

What is feng shui?

Feng shui is a Chinese practice around 3,000 years old, based on Taoist philosophy. Its name means "wind and water," and it focuses on arranging a space so that energy — "chi" — flows freely.

What is the difference between Vaastu and feng shui?

A popular analogy: Vaastu is like baking a cake — you get the ingredients right from the start, during construction. Feng shui is like decorating a cake — adjusting an existing space to improve its energy. Vaastu is direction/element-based; feng shui centres on the flow of chi.

Which is better, Vaastu or feng shui?

Neither is "better." Vaastu suits those building or wanting fixed, directional rules; feng shui suits those adjusting an existing home and working with energy flow. Choose what fits your beliefs and home.

Are the five elements the same in both?

Not exactly. Vaastu uses earth, water, fire, air and space. Feng shui uses wood, fire, earth, metal and water. Both aim to balance these elements within a space.

Does feng shui use a compass like Vaastu?

Some feng shui schools (the "compass" branch) use a luopan compass to read directions, much like Vaastu uses directions. Other schools focus on the "form" of the space rather than the compass.

What is "chi" or "qi" in feng shui?

Chi is the vital energy or life force that feng shui seeks to balance. The goal is to let chi flow smoothly through a space — not too fast, not too stagnant — for wellbeing and good fortune.

What is the bagua map?

The bagua ("eight areas") is a feng shui energy map that overlays a floor plan, linking each area of your home to a life area such as career, relationships, wealth or health.

Are Vaastu and feng shui religions?

No. Both are secular systems of arranging space, though they grew out of spiritual traditions. People of any faith — or none — use them as design philosophies.

Can I use both Vaastu and feng shui together?

Many people blend them, taking Vaastu's directional logic for a new build and feng shui's flow-based tweaks for an existing room. The key is intention and comfort, not strict adherence.

What are the five elements in feng shui?

Wood, fire, earth, metal and water. Feng shui balances these through colours, shapes and materials — for example, plants for wood, candles for fire, and a small fountain for water.

What does "wind and water" mean?

The name feng shui literally translates to "wind and water," symbolising the two invisible natural forces that shape a landscape — and, by extension, the energy of a home.

Does Vaastu apply to offices and shops too?

Yes. Vaastu is used for homes, offices, shops and factories. The same directional logic applies — for example keeping reception in the north-east and the owner in the south-west.

Is Vaastu only about directions?

Directions are central, but Vaastu also considers the five elements, the shape of the plot, room proportions and the placement of features like the well, tank and entrance.

Front Door, Bedroom & Bed Placement

The two areas people search about most: how energy enters the home, and where to sleep.

Why is the front door important in feng shui?

The front door is called the "mouth of chi" — the main entry point for energy. It is traditionally kept clean, well-lit, unblocked and able to open fully so positive energy can flow in.

Which direction should my front door face?

In Vaastu, east and north are favoured. In feng shui, the "best" direction depends on your personal kua number and the bagua, so there is no single universally lucky facing.

What should I avoid near the front door?

Avoid clutter, a blocked swing, broken items and a mirror directly facing the door. Keep the entry bright, welcoming and clear in both traditions.

Where should my bed be placed for good feng shui?

The "command position" is key: place the bed so you can see the door but are not directly in line with it, with a solid wall behind the headboard for support and stability.

Should the bed face a certain direction?

Feng shui focuses more on the command position than a strict compass direction. Vaastu traditionally favours sleeping with the head to the south or east and avoiding the north.

Is it bad to have the bed under a window?

Both traditions generally prefer a solid wall behind the bed rather than a window, as it is felt to give less support and a less restful sleep.

Can I have a mirror in the bedroom?

Yes, but avoid a mirror that directly reflects the bed — both traditions feel it can disturb rest. Place it inside a wardrobe or on a side wall instead.

What does my bedroom colour mean?

Calm, soft tones are recommended for rest in both traditions, drawing on the five elements and the bagua. Save bold, energetic colours for living or work spaces.

How do I feng shui a small bedroom?

Keep it clutter-free, ensure the bed has wall support, use soft lighting, and avoid storing items under the bed so energy can move. Even a small room benefits from a clear command position.

Where should a couple’s photos go?

Relationship photos suit the south-west (Vaastu, zone of stability) or the relationship area of the bagua. Keep them in pairs and in good condition.

What is a feng shui "cure" or enhancement?

A cure is a symbolic object — a mirror, plant, crystal, wind chime or light — used to adjust energy in a space. Practical fixes like light and airflow are usually more effective than decorative cures.

Is a home office good in the bedroom?

Both traditions advise keeping work out of the bedroom where possible, since the bedroom is for rest. If you must, use a screen or place the desk out of direct view of the bed.

Should the living room be in a certain direction?

Vaastu places the living room in the north, east or north-east — the open, social zones. Keep it clutter-free and welcoming, with good seating that faces inward rather than backing the door.

How does natural light affect home energy?

Both traditions prize natural light and fresh air, which are linked to positive energy and health. Brighten dark rooms and ventilate stale ones as a first, powerful step.

Wealth, Colours, Plants & Home Office

The most-searched practical questions about attracting prosperity and arranging work.

What is the wealth corner and how do I activate it?

In feng shui, the wealth area sits in the far-left corner of your home from the entrance (part of the bagua). Keep it clean, add symbols of abundance, healthy plants and colours like purple, gold or green.

Where should I keep a safe or important documents?

Vaastu places the locker in the north or south-west; feng shui puts valuables in the wealth corner. Both agree it should be sturdy, closed and in good condition.

Which colours attract money and calm?

Purple, green and gold are linked to wealth in feng shui, while Vaastu uses element-based colours by direction. Choose colours that also genuinely feel calm and clean to you.

Are plants good for feng shui?

Yes — healthy, lush plants are considered very auspicious, symbolising growth. Avoid dead or dying plants, and keep thorny varieties out of main living areas.

Should I add a water fountain or aquarium?

Moving water is considered auspicious in both traditions, especially in the north or north-east. Keep it clean and well-maintained, since stagnant water has the opposite effect.

Which direction should my home office desk face?

Feng shui favours a "command position" desk — you can see the door, with a wall behind you. Vaastu traditionally favours facing east or north while working.

Where should the kitchen stove go?

Vaastu places the kitchen in the south-east (fire zone). Feng shui advises the cook should be able to see the door and not have the stove directly opposite the sink (fire-water clash).

Is it bad to have the toilet in a certain area?

Both traditions prefer toilets away from the sacred/wealth zones — Vaastu avoids the north-east, and feng shui avoids the centre and wealth corner. Keep them clean, closed and ventilated.

How important is decluttering for energy?

Very — both traditions see clutter as blocked, stagnant energy. Clearing it is the single most recommended first step, and it also genuinely makes a home feel calmer.

Does the shape of my home matter?

A regular shape (square or rectangle) is easiest in both systems. Irregular or missing corners are "balanced" with plants, lights, mirrors or symbolic remedies where rebuilding is not possible.

Remedies, Doubts & Getting Started

The honest questions — does it work, and where do I begin?

Can I improve my home’s energy without renovating?

Absolutely. Lighting, airflow, decluttering, repair, plants and rearranging furniture are the most powerful and cheapest "remedies" in every tradition, and need no construction.

Do Vaastu and feng shui actually work?

Their traditional energy explanations are not scientifically proven. But the practices promote cleaner, brighter, better-ventilated, more organised homes — and those measurably feel better to live in.

How do I find my feng shui kua number?

Your kua number comes from your birth year and gender, and it points to your lucky and unlucky directions. Many feng shui practitioners and apps calculate it for you.

What is the command position and why does it matter?

It is the spot in a room diagonally opposite the door, with your back to a solid wall. Placing your bed or desk here is meant to give a sense of security and control.

Should I hire a consultant or learn myself?

You can start with the basics yourself — declutter, fix lighting, position your bed well. For a new build or a serious concern, an experienced consultant can offer personalised guidance.

Is feng shui expensive to apply?

Not at all. The highest-impact changes — decluttering, cleaning, lighting and moving furniture — are free. Expensive "cures" are rarely necessary.

Can Vaastu or feng shui help me sell my home?

A clean, bright, well-staged home photographs and shows better, which genuinely helps selling. The spiritual "energy" is a bonus; the practical staging does most of the work.

What is the single best thing I can do today?

Declutter your entrance and let in more light and fresh air. In every tradition this is the most recommended, most effective first step — and it works immediately.

Do I have to believe in it for it to help?

The practical benefits — tidiness, light, airflow, calm — work regardless of belief. Intention and consistent habits matter more than faith.

How can I learn more about my home’s Vaastu?

Start by mapping your home's directions and checking each room's placement. Apps like GreatOhm can scan your floor plan and give personalised, non-destructive guidance in one place.

Can Vaastu help with a rented apartment?

Yes — tenants can apply the non-destructive principles: declutter, brighten the entrance, position the bed and desk well, and keep the sacred zones clean, all without altering the structure.

Is a north-east extension or cut important to fix?

The north-east is the most sensitive zone in Vaastu. An extension there is usually seen as a defect; if you cannot rebuild, practitioners balance it with light, plants or symbolic remedies.
