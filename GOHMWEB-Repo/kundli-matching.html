Kundli Matching for Marriage — Guna Milan Explained

In Vedic tradition, kundli matching (Guna Milan) is a cherished step for couples considering marriage. GreatOhm compares two birth charts across the traditional compatibility factors and gives you a clear, compassionate analysis of the harmony between you.

What is Kundli Matching?

Kundli matching, also known as Guna Milan or Ashtakoota matching, is the centuries-old Vedic method of comparing the birth charts of a prospective bride and groom. The practice rests on the belief that the positions of the Moon and planets at birth influence temperament, values and life patterns — and that a compatible pairing encourages a harmonious marriage.

The Ashtakoota System — 36 Gunas

The most widely used system scores a match across eight categories, or kootas, worth 36 gunas in total. They are:

  • Varna (1 guna) — spiritual and professional temperament
  • Vashya (2 gunas) — mutual attraction and control
  • Tara (3 gunas) — longevity and destiny connection
  • Yoni (4 gunas) — physical and intimate compatibility
  • Graha Maitri (5 gunas) — mental compatibility and friendship
  • Gana (6 gunas) — temperament and nature
  • Bhakoot (7 gunas) — love and children prospects
  • Nadi (8 gunas) — health and hereditary compatibility

A score of 18 or above out of 36 is generally considered acceptable. Scores of 27 to 32 are regarded as excellent, and near-perfect scores indicate a deeply harmonious union. Scores below 18 are usually reviewed carefully, with remedial guidance from an astrologer.

What Beyond the 36 Gunas Matters?

Guna Milan is important, but it is only part of the picture. A complete marriage compatibility check also examines the placement of the 7th house (marriage), Venus (love and attraction), Jupiter (commitment), and the Moon's sign, along with Manglik and Kaal Sarp dosha considerations.
