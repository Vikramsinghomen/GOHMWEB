The 50 Questions Indians Ask Astrologers Most — Answered

Astrology By GreatOhm · Updated 2026-10-10

Across 2026, several studies analysed hundreds of thousands of real questions people in India put to astrologers and AI Jyotish tools — 10,432 typed questions at GrahAI, 351,000 conversations at Ishvaram, and over 1,400 at Kundli.me. The findings are striking: career now edges ahead of marriage, one question in four is really about when, and doshas like Mangal Dosh and Pitra Dosh dominate people's worries. Below are the 50 questions that come up again and again, grouped by theme, each answered plainly and without fear-mongering. Remember: astrology is used for guidance and self-reflection, never as a substitute for professional medical, financial or legal advice.

Career & Work (India’s #1 concern today)

Career now leads marriage in the data — around 39% of first questions are about work, and career is where "when" questions cluster most (nearly 39% of career questions ask about timing).

When will I get a job?

Astrologers look at your 10th house (karma bhava, career), its ruling planet, the current dasha and Saturn’s transit. A favourable dasha of the 10th lord or a supportive Jupiter/Saturn transit often marks an opening. Use that period as a window to apply, upskill and network actively.

Will I get a government (sarkari naukri) job?

Government-job "yoga" is linked to a strong Sun, the 6th house (service and competition) and Saturn. Studies show about 15% of career questions mention government jobs or exams like UPSC, SSC and banking. Astrologers usually suggest timing your attempt to a favourable dasha while you keep preparing steadily.

Business or job — which is better for me?

A strong 7th house and Mercury favour independent business; a strong 10th and 6th favour structured employment. Many charts show both, so the choice often comes down to your current dasha: a Mercury or Venus period can favour starting something of your own.

Is this the right time to change my job?

Astrologers compare the running dasha with that of your 10th lord. A change during a weak or afflicted period for your career lord is usually advised against; a supportive Jupiter transit over your 10th or ascendant is considered a good window.

Will I get a promotion or recognition at work?

The 11th house (gains) and the strength of the Sun matter here. A favourable Sun dasha or a Jupiter transit to the 10th/11th is traditionally seen as a period of visibility and reward for effort already put in.

Is working or settling abroad indicated in my chart?

"Videsh yoga" is read from the 12th house (foreign lands), the 9th (long journeys) and the influence of Rahu and Saturn. If present, a favourable period for the 12th lord or Rahu is often pointed to as the timing to pursue it.

Why do I keep getting rejected despite working hard?

Astrologers may look at afflictions to the 10th house or a difficult Saturn period, which classically tests patience. The traditional advice is to use the period to build skill and resilience, since Saturn rewards sustained effort over time.

Which career field suits me best?

Planets map to fields: Mercury to communication and commerce, Mars to engineering and defence, Venus to arts, Jupiter to teaching and law, Saturn to research. The strongest planet in your 10th house or as your 10th lord is usually the starting point.

Will my business succeed?

The 7th house (partnerships), 11th (profits) and Mercury (trade) are examined with the current dasha. A strong Mercury in a supportive period is considered favourable — paired with practical planning rather than a promise.

Marriage & Relationships

Still the dominant theme for people in their 20s — about 24–29% of questions, and around 35% of marriage questions are really "when will it happen?"

When will I get married?

Marriage timing is read from the 7th house, its lord, Venus (for men) / Jupiter (for women) and the Venus dasha. Astrologers point to a dasha or transit of the 7th lord or Venus as the most likely window rather than a fixed date.

Is there a delay in my marriage, and why?

Delays are attributed to Saturn afflicting the 7th house or its lord, a weak Venus, or a dosha like Mangal Dosh. Astrologers then look for when the affliction eases — usually the start of a favourable Venus or 7th-lord period.

Will it be a love marriage or an arranged marriage?

A strong 5th house (romance) with Rahu's influence often indicates love marriage; a strong 7th with traditional benefic influence leans arranged. About 11% of marriage questions weigh love versus arranged specifically.

Is my partner and I compatible (kundli matching)?

Kundli Milan compares the Moon signs of both partners using the Ashtakoota system — 36 points across eight factors. Astrologers look at the overall score plus specific factors like Mangal Dosh, Nadi and Bhakoot.

Does Mangal Dosh mean I can’t get married?

No. Mangal Dosh (Mars in the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house) is common and often cancels out — for example if Mars is in its own sign, or if both partners are Manglik. It is the single biggest dosha worry in the data, but it is rarely a real barrier.

Will my marriage be happy?

The condition of the 7th house, its lord and benefic aspects (especially from Jupiter) are examined. A well-placed 7th lord with Jupiter's blessing is considered a strong indicator of a stable, supportive marriage.

When will I meet my life partner?

Astrologers look at the dasha of the 7th lord, Venus, or the lord of the 5th/11th to time a meeting, often linking it to a period when Venus or the Moon is strongly activated.

Is a second marriage indicated in my chart?

This is read from afflictions to the 7th house, the influence of Rahu or a afflicted Venus, and the Navamsa (D9) chart. Astrologers treat it as a possibility to understand, not a certainty.

My relationship is struggling — will it survive?

Astrologers compare both charts (synastry) and the current dasha affecting the 7th house. Difficult transits are often seen as temporary phases to work through, with remedies and patience suggested.

How do I improve my relationship with my spouse?

Beyond chart analysis, astrologers suggest strengthening Venus (for harmony), observing simple remedies, and using favourable periods for important conversations. Practical communication always matters more than any gemstone.

Doshas & Remedies (India’s #3 concern)

Around 22% of first questions are about doshas. Mangal Dosh leads (25%), followed by Pitra Dosh (23%), Shani Dosh / Sade Sati (19%), Rahu (13%), Kaal Sarp (10%) and Ketu (9%).

Do I have Mangal Dosh, and how do I cancel it?

Check Mars' placement from the Moon, ascendant and Venus. It is considered cancelled if Mars is in its own or exaltation sign, after age 28, or if both partners are Manglik. Astrologers also suggest Kumbh Vivah, Hanuman Chalisa or Tuesday charity as traditional remedies.

What is Kaal Sarp Dosh and is it affecting me?

Formed when all seven planets fall between Rahu and Ketu. Many astrologers consider it influential rather than always harmful. Remedies include Shiva worship, Rahu-Ketu mantras and charity — but a full-chart reading matters more than the label.

What is Pitra Dosh and how do I remove it?

Linked to the Sun, Saturn and the 9th house, and to rites for ancestors. It is the second-biggest dosha worry. Traditional remedies include Shraddh ceremonies, feeding the needy, and worshipping Lord Shiva or Vishnu on specific days.

Am I going through Sade Sati right now?

Sade Sati is Saturn transiting the sign before, on, and after your Moon sign — about 7.5 years. Astrologers check where Saturn currently sits relative to your Janma Rashi to tell you which of the three phases you are in.

How do I reduce the effects of Shani (Saturn)?

Discipline, honesty and helping the underprivileged are the classic Shani remedies, along with Saturday charity, Shani mantra and wearing blue or black. Saturn rewards effort and patience above all else.

Does Rahu/Ketu Dosh affect my life?

Rahu and Ketu (the lunar nodes) are linked to sudden changes, obsession and karmic lessons. Astrologers read their house placement and aspects rather than treating the presence alone as bad.

Which gemstone should I wear?

Gemstones are chosen to strengthen a weak but benefic planet — for example blue sapphire for Saturn, yellow sapphire for Jupiter. Astrologers always recommend a trial period and warn against wearing a stone for an already-strong or malefic planet.

Are remedies really necessary, or is effort enough?

Most ethical astrologers say effort, timing and self-awareness come first; remedies are supportive. This is why a good reading focuses on your strengths and timing rather than fear.

Can my dosha be fixed completely?

Rather than "fixing," astrologers speak of reducing a dosha's impact through timing, lifestyle and traditional remedies, while you work on the practical side of the same problem.

Timing & Muhurat

One question in four is really about "when" — and timing lookups (panchang, muhurat, choghadiya) peak on Saturdays, Shani’s day.

What is a shubh muhurat?

A muhurat is an auspicious time window chosen from the Panchang, when the tithi, nakshatra, yoga and weekday align favourably. It is used for weddings, Griha Pravesh, business launches and vehicle purchases.

When is the best muhurat for my wedding?

Astrologers check the compatibility of both charts, then find a window when Venus and the 7th-lord are strong and no inauspicious transit (like Rahu Kaal) falls in the ceremony.

When should I buy a house or start construction?

The 4th house (property), its lord and a favourable Moon period are considered, along with a muhurat free of Rahu Kaal and other inauspicious periods from the Panchang.

When is a good time to start a new business?

A strong Mercury, a favourable 10th/11th-lord dasha and a muhurat aligned with your ascendant's favourable periods are traditionally chosen for a launch.

What is Rahu Kaal and should I avoid it?

Rahu Kaal is a roughly 90-minute inauspicious period each day, varying by weekday. Tradition advises avoiding starting new ventures or ceremonies during it.

What is the difference between a muhurat and panchang?

The Panchang is the daily Vedic calendar of five elements (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and weekday). A muhurat is a specific auspicious window chosen from it for a particular event.

Money, Education, Health & Life

Money is a steady undercurrent (2–7% of questions), alongside education and, rising with age, health.

When will my financial situation improve?

The 2nd house (savings), 11th (gains) and their lords, plus the dasha of the wealth-giving planets, are examined. A favourable Jupiter or Venus period is often linked to improvement.

Should I invest in the stock market?

Astrologers may check the strength of Mercury, Rahu and the 5th/11th houses (speculation and gains). But this is for reflection only — never a substitute for qualified financial advice.

Will I become wealthy?

Dhana yogas are combinations of the 2nd, 5th, 9th and 11th houses and their benefic lords. Their presence suggests potential; effort and smart decisions still do the work.

Which subject or stream should I choose?

Mercury favours commerce and communication, Mars engineering, Venus arts, Jupiter law and philosophy. The strongest planet in the 4th/5th house usually guides the choice.

Will I do well in my exams?

The 4th/5th houses, Moon and Mercury are examined along with the transit during the exam period. Astrologers pair this with a reminder that preparation is the real variable.

What does my chart say about my health?

The 6th house (illness), ascendant (vitality) and afflicted planets are studied. This is for self-awareness only — always consult a qualified doctor for any health concern.

Will I have children, and when?

The 5th house (children), Jupiter and the Putra Karaka are examined, with the dasha of the 5th lord often used for timing.

What is my life purpose?

Read from the Sun (soul), the ascendant lord and the Nodes (Rahu/Ketu) which point to karmic growth areas. Many astrologers frame this as a direction, not a fixed destiny.

Will my parents support my career choice?

The 9th house (father/fortune), 4th (mother/home) and the Moon are checked. If supportive planets aspect the 10th, astrologers read it as a sign family will come around with communication.

How do I improve my relationship with my parents?

The Sun (father) and Moon (mind/emotions) condition, plus the 4th/9th houses, are examined. The 9th house also relates to the 4th, so strengthening one often eases the other.

When will I own my own house?

The 4th house, its lord, Mars (property) and a favourable Mars or Moon dasha are examined, with timing tied to a supportive period for the 4th lord.

Should I buy a vehicle now?

A vehicle is read from Venus and the 4th house, timed to a favourable Venus dasha and a muhurat free of Rahu Kaal and other inauspicious windows.

Will my court case or dispute be resolved?

The 6th house (litigation, enemies) and its lord are studied; astrologers look for when the 6th lord's difficult period eases. This is for reflection only — always follow your lawyer's advice.

When will my health improve?

The 6th house (disease), ascendant and the dasha of the affected planet are examined, with timing tied to the easing of an affliction. A doctor's care always comes first.

What does this year hold for me?

This is the annual Varshphal reading — comparing the Sun's return chart and the year's transits (especially Saturn, Jupiter and Rahu) against your birth chart.

How do I know if I am on the right path?

Astrologers compare your current dasha and transits to your chart's strengths, using the Nodes as a compass. The honest answer is usually a direction to test, not a certainty to wait for.
