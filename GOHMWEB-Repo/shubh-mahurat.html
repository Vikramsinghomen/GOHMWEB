Shubh Mahurat Finder — Choose the Right Moment

Every important beginning — a wedding, a house warming, a journey, a business launch — can be timed with care. GreatOhm's Shubh Mahurat Finder suggests the best days and time windows from accurate panchang data.

What Makes a Time Auspicious?

In Vedic tradition, the quality of a moment is read through the panchang — five limbs of time: tithi (lunar day), vara (weekday), nakshatra (constellation), yoga and karana. When these combine favourably, the moment is considered a mahurat.

How the Finder Works

GreatOhm computes a full panchang for the dates you are considering, then filters the suitable windows based on the event type:

  • Marriage: favours fertile nakshatras and maghā, swati and other union-friendly constellations
  • House warming (Griha Pravesh): avoids Rahu Kalam and uses stabilising tithis and nakshatras
  • Travel (Shoola): checks shoola direction and favourable weekday combinations
  • Business and purchases: balances tithi, weekday and planetary hours

Panchang & Daily Mahurat

You can use the app's daily panchang to see today's tithi, nakshatra, elevations and Rahu Kalam directly. When a fixed date is unavoidable (for example, a booked venue), the finder still highlights the best window within that day so you can plan around it.

Personalised vs General Mahurat

A general mahurat is good for most people, but the most precise approach also considers the couple's or family's birth Moon signs. GreatOhm lets you pair the general finder with your kundli-based preferences, and you can confirm the final timing with Pandit Ji in the app.
