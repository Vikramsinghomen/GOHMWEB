Lo Shu Grid — Chinese Magic Square Numerology

The Lo Shu Grid is a 3x3 magic square that has been used for centuries to read personality and balance through numbers. GreatOhm plots your birth date onto the grid and shows you exactly what is present — and what is missing.

The Origin of the Lo Shu Grid

Legend traces the Lo Shu square to a turtle emerging from the Luo River in ancient China, with its shell marked by numbers arranged so every row, column and diagonal sums to 15. Feng Shui and Chinese numerology adopted the square as a map of energy, assigning each cell an element, a direction and a set of meanings.

How Your Grid Is Built

GreatOhm writes your full birth date as a single string of digits and places each one into its cell. Because the grid is fixed, numbers repeat and accumulate in certain cells while other cells stay empty. The result — which numbers are strong, repeated, present or absent — becomes the blueprint for your reading.

Reading Present and Missing Numbers

Present numbers reflect active traits: for instance, the central 5 relates to balance and decision, the 3 and 4 diagonal to creativity and hard work, and the 6 and 1 line to reward and recognition. Missing numbers point to qualities that need conscious development — like focus, confidence or organisation. The grid also maps onto the five elements: water, earth, fire, wood and metal.

Balancing the Grid

A missing number is not a verdict — it is an invitation. Practical suggestions include wearing or displaying the missing digit, choosing the related element's colours, adapting your name or brand numbers, and mindful daily habits. GreatOhm ties these suggestions back to your numerology dashboard, next to your life path and daily numerology.
