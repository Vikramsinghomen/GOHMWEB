Manglik Dosha Check — Mangal Dosha & Kaal Sarp Dosha

Mangal Dosha and Kaal Sarp Dosha are two of the most commonly asked-about conditions in matrimonial astrology. GreatOhm checks your kundli for both, explains what they mean, and suggests remedies that match your chart.

What is Mangal Dosha (Manglik Dosha)?

Manglik Dosha arises when Mars (Mangal) occupies the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house from the Moon sign or ascendant in the birth chart. In Vedic tradition this placement is reviewed before marriage because Mars is associated with energy, conflict and protection — and, in an unfavourable position, with turbulence in relationships.

How the Manglik Check Works

GreatOhm analyses the exact position of Mars in your natal chart relative to both your Moon sign (rashi) and lagna. It then classifies you as non-Manglik or Manglik, and where relevant, shows the degree of dosha. The same chart is also examined for Kaal Sarp Dosha — the formation where all seven planets sit between Rahu and Ketu.

Does Manglik and Kaal Sarp Mean Unlucky?

No. Doshas are patterns that need awareness, not verdicts of doom. A person can be Manglik and enjoy a happy marriage, especially when the dosha is cancelled or remedied. Similarly, Kaal Sarp Dosha varies greatly in intensity — in some charts it is more a karmic theme than a hardship.

Dosha Cancellation & Remedies

Traditional cancellations include marrying another Manglik, performing Kumbh Vivah, or following chart-specific remedies chosen by an astrologer. Chanting the Hanuman Chalisa, Mars-related mantras such as "Om Angarakaya Namah", and specific poojas are commonly suggested.
