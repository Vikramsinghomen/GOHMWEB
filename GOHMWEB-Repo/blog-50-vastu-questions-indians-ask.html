The 50 Vaastu Questions Indians Ask Most — Answered

Vastu By GreatOhm · Updated 2026-10-10

Vaastu is the question Indian families ask the moment they buy, build or rent a home. Which direction should the main door face? Is a south-facing plot really bad? Where should the kitchen, bedroom and water tank go — and can any of it be fixed without breaking walls? Drawing on the questions practitioners answer most often (from Vastu Shastra Guru's 51 FAQs to the beginner guides and classical Vedic Vastu texts), here are the 50 that come up again and again — answered calmly, with the important reminder that local building rules, budget and safety always come before any traditional guideline.

Vaastu Basics & Common Myths

What Vaastu is, who it applies to, and the worries that come up first.

What is Vaastu Shastra?

Vaastu Shastra is the ancient Indian science of architecture and spatial harmony. It guides how a home is designed and arranged so that it feels balanced with nature and the five elements, supporting wellbeing and prosperity.

What are the five elements of Vaastu?

Earth, water, fire, air and space (ether). Vaastu aims to keep these five elements balanced in every part of a home, since each direction and room is associated with one of them.

Is Vaastu only for India, or does it work worldwide?

Vaastu is applied all over the world, and its directional rules stay the same regardless of country — only the local climate and building codes change.

Is Vaastu specific to any religion?

No. Vaastu is a secular science of space and direction. People of any religion follow it, since it deals with architecture and energy rather than worship.

Does Vaastu affect children too?

Practitioners say Vaastu affects everyone in the home, regardless of age, because it is about the energy of the space itself rather than any one person.

I live in a rented house — does its Vaastu affect me?

Yes, traditionally. Vaastu is believed to affect whoever occupies a space, so tenants can apply simple, non-destructive remedies just as owners do.

How long does it take to see Vaastu results?

There is no fixed timeline. Practitioners usually say it depends on the defects and the changes made — and that good habits (cleanliness, light, airflow) improve a home's feel almost immediately.

Can Vaastu remedies remove a defect completely?

A remedy is meant to reduce the ill-effect of a defect, not erase it. Serious structural issues are best corrected during construction or renovation.

Is Vaastu scientific?

Many of its principles overlap with good design — sunlight, ventilation, ventilation of stale air, clutter control and sensible room placement. The traditional "energy" explanations are not scientifically proven, but the practical outcomes often feel genuinely better.

Is Vaastu the same as feng shui?

They share a goal — harmonious space — but differ in origin and method. Vaastu is Indian and direction/element-based, tied to construction; feng shui is Chinese and focuses on the flow of "chi" and adjusting an existing space.

Main Door, Directions & Plot

The first things buyers check: which facing is good, and where the entrance should be.

Which direction should the main door face?

East and North entrances are generally preferred in Vaastu because they invite morning sunlight and are linked to prosperity. The exact auspicious position depends on the house facing and a grid-based calculation.

Is a south-facing house really bad in Vaastu?

No — a south-facing home is not automatically bad. It is treated as a "stronger" exposure, so the focus is on keeping the south and south-west heavier (storage, staircase, master bedroom) and the north and north-east lighter and open.

Is a west-facing plot good or bad?

West-facing homes can be fine when planned well. The key is balancing the zones rather than avoiding the direction — many successful homes face west.

Which facing is considered the most auspicious?

East-facing is traditionally the most favoured for the main entrance, associated with the rising sun, health and new beginnings. But a well-balanced home of any facing can work well.

What should I keep near the main entrance?

Keep it clean, bright, clutter-free and welcoming — good lighting, a smooth-working door, and a pleasant first view. Avoid shoe clutter, broken items and damp corners at the threshold.

Should I place a mirror facing the main door?

Many traditions advise against a big mirror directly facing the main door, as it is thought to push entering energy back out. If you want a mirror, place it on a side wall instead.

Is a plot with roads on all four sides good?

Yes — a plot with roads on all four sides is traditionally considered the best, since it allows light and air from every direction.

Is it bad to buy a plot lower than the road?

A plot lower than the surrounding ground tends to collect water and damp, so practitioners advise raising it to the surrounding level to avoid practical and Vaastu problems.

What does it mean if my plot slopes north to south?

A north-high, south-low slope is traditionally seen as a defect linked to financial strain. If you already own such a plot, practitioners suggest correcting the level with proper drainage.

How many doors should a house have?

Tradition favours an even number of doors, and it is best to keep the total modest and balanced, with the main door clearly the most prominent.

Kitchen, Bedroom & Sleeping Direction

The two rooms people worry about most after the entrance.

Where should the kitchen be placed?

The south-east (the fire zone, ruled by Agni) is the ideal spot for the kitchen, so the cook can face east while cooking. North-east and north-west are alternatives in some traditions.

Which direction should I face while cooking?

Facing east while cooking is traditionally considered best, as it is linked to health and nourishment. The key is also keeping the cooking area clean, ventilated and well-lit.

Can the kitchen and toilet be next to each other?

Vaastu advises against a kitchen sharing a common wall with a toilet or bathroom, both for hygiene and traditional reasons. If your flat is fixed, keep the shared wall sealed and ventilated.

Where should the master bedroom be?

The south-west is traditionally best for the master bedroom, as it is the heaviest, most stable zone and supports rest and authority. Children's rooms suit the north-west or west.

Which direction should I sleep in?

Sleeping with your head to the south is traditionally considered most restful, followed by the east. Practitioners usually advise against sleeping with the head to the north.

Should the bed be against a wall?

Yes — a solid wall behind the bed (your headboard side) is recommended for a sense of support and stability, rather than having the bed under a window or beam.

Is it bad to have a beam over the bed?

A beam directly over the bed is traditionally avoided as it is felt to create pressure. If you cannot move the bed, a false ceiling is the usual practical remedy.

Can I keep a mirror in the bedroom?

Mirrors are fine in a bedroom, but traditionally they should not directly face the bed. Place them on a wardrobe or a wall to the side instead.

Where should children study?

A study in the east or north zone with the desk facing east or north is traditionally recommended for focus. Keep it bright, organised and away from distractions.

Is it okay to have a bedroom in the north-east?

The north-east is best kept light and open, so it is not ideal for the master bedroom. It suits a study, prayer space or an infrequently used guest room instead.

Pooja Room, Water Tank, Toilet & Staircase

The specific placements Indian families ask about most.

Where should the pooja (prayer) room be?

The north-east is the traditional spot for the pooja room, as it is the calmest, most sacred zone. Keep it clean, well-lit and free of clutter, with the idols facing west or east so you face them while praying.

Which direction should the idols face?

Idols are generally placed so that they face west or east, allowing worshippers to face east or west respectively. Keep them off the floor and away from the bathroom wall.

Where should the overhead water tank be?

The overhead tank is best placed in the south-west, west or south, since it is heavy and should sit over the heavier zones. An underground tank suits the north-east.

Where should the underground water tank go?

An underground water tank is traditionally placed in the north-east, keeping the light, open zones above it free of heavy storage.

Which direction is best for the toilet and bathroom?

Toilets and bathrooms are best in the west or north-west, or the south of south-west. Vaastu advises keeping the north-east free of toilets, as it is the sacred zone.

Does a toilet in the north-east create a dosha?

Many consider a toilet in the north-east a defect to correct if possible. If the flat is fixed, keep the door closed, the area dry and well-ventilated, and use simple remedies like salt or a mirror.

Where should the staircase be?

Staircases are traditionally placed in the south, west or south-west — the heavy zones. A staircase in the exact centre (Brahmasthan) is usually avoided.

Is a staircase in the centre really bad?

A central staircase is traditionally discouraged because it is felt to disturb the Brahmasthan, the calm heart of the home. If you cannot move it, keep the area around it bright and uncluttered.

Can I keep a safe or locker for valuables?

The locker is traditionally placed in the north (the zone of Kuber, god of wealth) or the south/south-west, opening towards the north to symbolise inflow of abundance.

Where is the best place for an aquarium?

An aquarium or water feature is traditionally placed in the north or north-east, as water in the wealth zone is considered auspicious. Keep it clean and well-maintained.

Remedies Without Demolition & Common Doubts

Most people already live in a built home — so the practical question is what can be fixed without breaking walls.

Can Vaastu defects be fixed without demolition?

Yes. Many defects can be reduced with non-destructive remedies — better lighting and ventilation, rearranging furniture, using mirrors, plants, salt, and clearing the Brahmasthan (centre) — without breaking any walls.

What is the Brahmasthan and why keep it clear?

The Brahmasthan is the central zone of the home, considered its energetic heart. Vaastu advises keeping it open, light and clutter-free, and avoiding heavy objects, toilets or pillars there.

Do pyramids and crystals really fix Vaastu?

Some people use pyramids, crystals and yantras as symbolic remedies. They can support a positive mindset, but practical fixes — light, airflow, decluttering and repair — always come first.

My flat has a cut in the north-east corner. What now?

A cut or missing corner is traditionally seen as a defect. If you cannot rebuild it, practitioners often symbolically "complete" it with a suitable remedy or by placing a representation of the missing element.

Is an extended south-west good or bad?

An extended south-west is generally considered favourable, since the south-west is the zone of stability. An extended north-east, by contrast, is usually seen as a defect to correct.

Which plants are good for the home in Vaastu?

Tulsi (holy basil), money plant and other healthy green plants are considered auspicious, especially in the north and east. Avoid thorny plants (except roses) indoors and remove dead plants.

Should I place a wall clock or photos as per Vaastu?

Small items like clocks are best kept running and away from the sacred north-east. Family photos suit the south-west or west, the zones of relationships and stability.

How do I find which direction my house faces?

Stand at the main entrance facing outwards with a compass, or use a compass app. The direction you face is your house's facing, which anchors the whole Vaastu grid.

Should I consult a Vaastu expert or use an app?

For major construction, an experienced consultant is valuable. For an existing home, apps like GreatOhm can scan your floor plan and flag issues quickly, then you can seek expert help for anything serious.

Will following Vaastu guarantee wealth or success?

No. Vaastu is a traditional framework, not a guarantee. What it reliably offers is a cleaner, better lit, better ventilated home — and those genuinely support comfort, focus and wellbeing.
