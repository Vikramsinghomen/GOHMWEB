What is Vastu Shastra?

Vastu Shastra is the ancient Indian science of architecture and spatial harmony. Rooted in the belief that your environment influences your wellbeing, Vastu offers guidance on how to arrange your home and office so that positive energy flows freely — bringing balance to health, relationships and prosperity.

Vastu for Your Home

GreatOhm provides practical, easy-to-apply Vastu tips for every part of your home — from the position of your bed and kitchen to the colours and directions that suit your space best. Simple adjustments can make a remarkable difference to the feel of your living environment.

Vastu for Your Office & Work

Your workspace deserves harmony too. GreatOhm offers Vastu guidance for offices, studies and workspaces, helping you create an environment that supports focus, clarity and productivity.

Personalised Remedies & Gemstones

No two homes — or people — are alike. GreatOhm tailors its Vastu remedies to your personal planetary profile, including personalised gemstone recommendations to support your goals and balance the energies around you.
