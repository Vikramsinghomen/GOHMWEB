Kundli App — Your Vedic Birth Chart, Explained

Your birth chart is a cosmic snapshot of the moment you arrived. GreatOhm turns your Kundli into clear, personal insight — with dashas, yogas, matching and muhurat at your fingertips.

What is a Kundli?

A Kundli, or Vedic birth chart, is a map of planetary positions at the exact moment of your birth. For thousands of years, Vedic astrologers have used this map to understand personality, predict life events and guide important decisions. Your Kundli is as unique as your fingerprint.

Generate & Analyse Your Birth Chart

GreatOhm makes generating your Kundli effortless. Simply enter your birth date, time and place, and the app builds your complete Vedic chart — including planetary dashas, yogas and deep interpretations, all explained in language you can actually understand.

Kundli Matching for Marriage

In Vedic tradition, Kundli matching (or Guna Milan) is a cherished step for couples considering marriage. GreatOhm compares two charts across the traditional compatibility factors and offers a clear, compassionate analysis — helping you understand the harmony between you.

Auspicious Muhurat

Timing matters. GreatOhm helps you find auspicious muhurat timings for important events — weddings, housewarmings, new beginnings and more — so you can start your journey aligned with the stars.

Private & Secure by Design

Your birth details are deeply personal. GreatOhm encrypts your data, keeps it on your device, and uses it only to deliver the services you request. Privacy isn't a feature — it's the foundation.
